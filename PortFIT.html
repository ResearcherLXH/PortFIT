PortFIT · Port Footprint and Infrastructure planning Tool for hydrogen and e-fuels

Describe a port and its future fuel demand, choose how each fuel reaches the port and where it goes, and see the facilities this requires and the land they take — inside the port fence, in safety zones, and for renewable power outside the fence.

How to use: start in Module 1 Port context, work through Modules 1–4, and read the results in Module 5 Land by system boundary. Module 0 Overview summarises your inputs and results.

Version 1.0-beta · November 2026
Beta version for testing. PortFIT is a screening tool: many parameters are placeholders and safety distances are illustrative, so do not use the results for permitting or investment decisions. Please work through the tester tasks and send your feedback.

Infrastructure requirements

facilities and sizing

Each active option becomes facilities. Open a row to see its equation and parameters; equation numbers (E1–E50) follow the sizing model in the design documentation. Each sizing box shows reference values for its parameters, taken from the sources listed under References; adjust them if you have more specific or accurate values for your port. Red figures are the safety zone around a facility; blue notes are water area.

Electrolyser footprint basis

An electrolyser is sized only when the source includes on-site production (SRC.6); the other sources deliver hydrogen or its derivatives already made. Plant-level factors include the balance of plant (BoP): water supply and treatment, power conversion, cooling and compression. With “stack and building only”, water treatment land is added separately.

Land by system boundary

All fuels together. Median and P10–P90 range from a seeded Monte Carlo over the parameter ranges.

FacilityLand (ha)Zone (ha)
Land is the facility’s own plot. Zone is the safety zone around it beyond that plot, π(r + R)² − A with r = √(A/π) and R the safety distance; it counts in the “+ safety zones” boundary only. On-site production (SRC.6) is the electrolyser plant inside the fence; SRC.1–5 are arrival modes and hold no land.
